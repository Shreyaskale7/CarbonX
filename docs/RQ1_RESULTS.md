# RQ1 results: when does a grid-factor change alone cross 5%?

_Generated by `scripts/rq1_materiality.py` from `data/cea/factors.csv`. Do not edit by hand._

Factor: `grid.weighted_avg_incl_re_captive.incl_imports` (all-India, the series CarbonX uses). If grid electricity is a share *s* of the emissions in the GEI basis, a factor change *d* moves the total by *s·d*, crossing the 5% materiality threshold (SBTi) when *s* ≥ 5% / |*d*|.

**Basis, not inventory.** Per BEE's procedure (p. 7 note, to be confirmed against the notified text), 2024-27 targets count only combustion and indirect (electricity) emissions, not process emissions. So *s* is the Scope 2 share of *that* basis, which is larger than the share of a full Scope 1+2 inventory for cement, steel and refining. See `docs/RQ3_FINDINGS.md`.

## Four kinds of factor change

| kind | what changes | d | Scope 2 share needed to cross 5% |
|---|---|---|---|
| revision | largest restatement of one FY (FY2020-21), first vs latest version | -0.78% | never (would need >100%) |
| step | FY2025-26 (v22) vs FY2024-25 (v22) | -4.93% | never (would need >100%) |
| latest | BEE 'latest' before v22 (v21, FY2024-25) vs after (v22, FY2025-26) | -4.87% | never (would need >100%) |
| series | spread of CEA's four average series, v22 FY2025-26 | 21.85% | 23% |

## Reading it

- **CEA's restatements of a past year are small**: revision alone does not cross 5% at any Scope 2 share for this series. Base-year recalculation triggered purely by CEA revising the base year's own factor is immaterial here.
- **Which reading of 'latest', and the year-on-year step, are both just under 5%**: alone, they cross only at Scope 2 shares near 100%. But they are the same size as the 1–3% GEI targets, which is RQ2's point, not RQ1's.
- **The choice of series is the material one**: CEA's average series disagree by about 22%, so an entity whose basis is more than about a quarter grid electricity crosses 5% from the series choice alone. BEE's procedure does not name the series (RQ3).
